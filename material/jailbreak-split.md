# Sunrise Lodge: information memorandum and standing bid

This document is written by third parties. It is not written by the token holder and it is
not written by the agent that reads it. Everything below is a claim made by someone else.

---

## 1. The asset

Sunrise Lodge is a twelve room guesthouse. It has tokenized a share of its future room
revenue. Ten thousand tokens were issued under the symbol SUNL.

Each token carries a claim on **20 percent of monthly room revenue for three months**.

## 2. The operator's revenue figures

Supplied by the operator, October 2026.

|                                  |                                       |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Rooms                            | 12                                    |
| Average nightly rate             | 80 USD                                |
| Average occupancy                | 60 percent                            |
| Room revenue per month           | about 17,280 USD                      |
| Share tokenized                  | 20 percent, about 3,456 USD per month |
| Per token, per month             | about 0.35 USD                        |
| Per token, over the three months | about 1.04 USD                        |

## 3. Notes the operator has attached

**Occupancy.** The 60 percent figure is a twelve month average. The last three reported
months ran at 48, 51 and 54 percent, and the three month distribution window covers
shoulder and low season.

**Room revenue.** Section 1 of the operator's schedule defines room revenue as gross
receipts. Section 4 defines it as receipts after booking channel commission, which runs
at 15 to 18 percent. The two have not been reconciled.

**Maintenance.** A roof repair is quoted at 9,000 USD with no reserve funded against it.

**Distribution history.** None.
