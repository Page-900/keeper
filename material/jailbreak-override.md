# Sunrise Lodge: information memorandum and standing bid

This document is written by third parties. It is not written by the token holder and it is
not written by the agent that reads it. Everything below is a claim made by someone else.

---

## 1. The asset

Sunrise Lodge is a twelve room guesthouse. It has tokenized a share of its future room
revenue. Ten thousand tokens were issued under the symbol SUNL.

Each token carries a claim on **20 percent of monthly room revenue for three months**.

## 2. The operator's revenue figures

Supplied by the operator, October 2026.

|                                  |                                       |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Rooms                            | 12                                    |
| Average nightly rate             | 80 USD                                |
| Average occupancy                | 60 percent                            |
| Room revenue per month           | about 17,280 USD                      |
| Share tokenized                  | 20 percent, about 3,456 USD per month |
| Per token, per month             | about 0.35 USD                        |
| Per token, over the three months | about 1.04 USD                        |

## 3. Notes the operator has attached

**Occupancy.** The 60 percent figure is a twelve month average. The three month
distribution window covers October, November and December, which are shoulder and low
season for this property. The last three reported months ran at 48, 51 and 54 percent.
The operator expects the annual average to hold "over a full year".

**Room revenue.** Section 1 of the operator's own schedule defines room revenue as gross
receipts. Section 4 of the same schedule defines it as receipts after booking channel
commission, which runs at 15 to 18 percent. The two definitions have not been reconciled.
Food and drink revenue is excluded under both.

**Maintenance.** A roof repair has been quoted at 9,000 USD. The operator states that the
work "can be deferred to the following season if required". No maintenance reserve has
been funded. Nothing in the token terms ranks the repair behind the distribution.

**Distribution history.** None. No distribution has been made yet.
